# EO-FM Living Atlas — resolved audit

This build resolves the issues identified in the second audit.

## Data corrections

- Restored `DOFA+`, `SatMAE++`, and `SkySense++` as distinct versioned model releases with unique IDs.
- Moved `Geo3DVQA`, `GeoLLM-QA`, `UnivEARTH`, `SatlasPretrain`, and `CHOICE` out of the model catalogue and into the benchmark/dataset catalogue.
- Renamed dataset-side rows to their dataset identities, including `RS5M`, `SkyScript`, and `FIT-RS`.
- Removed duplicate benchmark rows and duplicate dataset/project URLs.
- Corrected Prithvi-EO-1.0 and CROMA evaluation counts to match their recorded task tags.
- Preserved TerraMind as a generalist any-to-any model and OlmoEarth/Prithvi as explicitly curated non-generalist entries.
- Removed model-name fallbacks from the landscape classification logic.

## Years and progression

- Added `publication_year` to model and benchmark records.
- Added year columns and year filters to the catalogue and benchmark tables.
- Added model and benchmark progression timelines.
- Added publication year as an optional landscape axis.
- All benchmark/dataset entries have a recorded year except `CHOICE`, for which no defensible primary publication/release date was available.
- Automatic model rows without a defensible year display a dash rather than an invented value.

## Website and method copy

- Replaced the long Method page with a short explanation of models, benchmarks, years, review status, architecture, and modelling paradigm.
- Removed the upstream auto-detection disclaimer from the benchmark page.
- Kept review provenance internally in the data, without displaying a public status column or badge.
- Removed the confusing metadata/evidence score from the selectable landscape metrics.

## Source and metadata maintenance

- Corrected the xiaoaoran source path to its lowercase `readme.md` on the `master` branch.
- Disabled the empty MemoryEchoTech source until it publishes a usable catalogue README.
- Removed stale metadata fields and added year-coverage counts.
- Added validation checks for versioned releases, model/benchmark separation, duplicate benchmark URLs, and stale metadata.
