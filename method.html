About the atlas

How this atlas is created

A transparent workflow for discovering, verifying, and maintaining EO foundation-model resources.

The EO-FM Living Atlas combines curated records with automated discovery to track Earth observation foundation models, benchmarks, datasets, and versioned releases.

Semantic paper map

Paper abstracts are embedded, clustered in the original embedding space, and projected to two dimensions with UMAP. Nearby points indicate related research themes, not model performance.

Step 1

Discover

Candidate entries are identified from research papers, official repositories, model hubs, project pages, community-maintained lists, and user submissions.

Step 2

Verify and classify

Entries are checked against primary sources and classified as foundation models, benchmarks or datasets, surveys, or other research resources. Only named foundation models and distinct releases appear in the main catalogue.

Step 3

Enrich and update

Available sources are used to record publication year, modalities, architecture, modelling paradigm, downstream tasks, and access links. Stronger evidence replaces incomplete or automatically inferred fields.

A living, non-exhaustive resource. Missing releases and corrections can be submitted through the Add entry page.
