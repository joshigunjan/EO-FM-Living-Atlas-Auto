Method

How the atlas is organized

A compact guide to the fields used in the catalogue.

Models

The catalogue contains reusable EO foundation models and versioned releases. Benchmarks, datasets, surveys, and application-only papers are kept outside the model table.

Benchmarks and datasets

The benchmark tab contains evaluation suites, pre-training datasets, embedding resources, and named datasets. Duplicate links and model-only rows are removed.

Architecture

The concrete network design, such as a Vision Transformer, masked autoencoder, Mamba/state-space model, encoder–decoder, or mixture of experts.

Modelling paradigm

The learning strategy or functional family, such as masked reconstruction, joint embedding/contrastive learning, vision–language learning, state-space modelling, or generative multimodality.

Publication year

The year of the first public paper or model release. It supports year filters and progression views; unknown years are shown as a dash.

Architecture vs paradigm: architecture describes what the model is built from; paradigm describes how it learns or what kind of representation system it is.
